# Rewriting the Code UK&I Career Summit 2026

## Talk Title

### What Is DevRel and Can AI Help Me Get There?

## Abstract

How does someone actually end up in DevRel? In this talk, I'll share my journey into developer relations: the unexpected turns, the skills that mattered most, and what I wish I knew starting out. I'll close with a live demo of how I use AI in my day-to-day work to build faster, learn quicker, and ship with confidence. You'll leave with a clearer picture of what DevRel really looks like and practical AI workflows you can try yourself.
